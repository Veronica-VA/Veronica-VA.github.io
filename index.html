Verónica Vázquez Aceves

Gravitational Wave Astrophysics

Hi! I'm Verónica Vázquez Aceves. I was born in Mexico, and since I was a kid, I was eager to understand how the universe works. I studied physics at the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM), where I first learned about gravitational waves (GWs) and immediately knew I wanted to work on them. I got a Master's in Astrophysics, also at UNAM, and later moved to Beijing to do a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics at the Chinese Academy of Sciences (focusing on GWs). After that, I joined the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics at Peking University (PKU) as a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow, where I continued working on GW astrophysics, especially on sources for the future space-based detectors such as LISA, TianQin, and Taiji. Currently, I'm a visiting researcher at the International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP-AP) in Beijing. In early 2027, I will join the Nicolaus Copernicus Astronomical Center (CAMK) in Warsaw, supported by the SONATINA‑10 fellowship, where I’ll keep investigating how GWs help us decode the universe’s most extreme phenomena.
